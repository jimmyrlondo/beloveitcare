About

A passion and readiness to serve.

Be Love It Care Agency is built on a simple idea: every person deserves support that matches who they are.

Our Story

Care, shaped around each person.

Many people with disabilities have unique needs and capabilities, and need services tailored to them. At Be Love It Care, you choose exactly what works — whether that's reliable transportation to a workplace, help preparing healthy meals at home, building skills for independent living, or simply having a trusted companion through the day.

We're based in Eastlake, Ohio (34734 Vine Street, Eastlake, OH 44095) and serve families across the state — partnering with county boards, SSAs, and other providers to deliver the right mix of support under DODD waiver plans.

Our company is also working to expand its offerings through a nonprofit program. For details, reach out to us today.
