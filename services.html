Services

In-home or community assistance for your clients.

Be Love It Care provides a wide range of services locally in Eastlake, OH and with our partners throughout Ohio. Our team of compassionate professionals work tirelessly to meet each person's unique needs.

Call (216) 971-1023 Ask a question
01

Non-Medical Transportation

Reliable rides to work, day programs, school, volunteer activities, and appointments. Per-trip or per-mile rates.

02

Adult Day Support

Build community connections, life skills, and independence through daily activities. Personal care included when needed.

03

Vocational Habilitation

Pre-vocational support that builds work readiness — attendance, communication, problem-solving, and workplace skills.

04

Money Management

Help with paying bills, writing checks, and tracking income and benefits — with training to build long-term skills.

05

Functional Behavioral Assessment

An assessment that uncovers the “why” behind challenging behaviors and guides a plan to support safer choices.

06

Social Work

Counseling and coordination that build self-help and adaptive skills and ease stress at work, home, or in family life.

07

Residential Respite

Short-term care that gives primary caregivers a break — authorized by an SSA, with daily and 15-minute rate options.

08

Career Planning

Help finding community jobs that fit — from identifying interests to landing a placement.

09

Individual Employment Support

On-the-job coaching, task training, and assistive technology to help a person learn and grow in their role.

10

Shared Living

Adults with developmental disabilities live with a family member or caregiver who provides 20%+ of their daily support.

11

Remote Support

Off-site monitoring through live two-way video and sensors — always paired with assistive technology.

12

Home Personal Care

Bathing, dressing, meals, mobility, and daily living support — delivered in the comfort of home.

13

Community Respite

Backup support from other caregivers in community settings — rec centers, camps, and organized programs.

14

Supported Living & Community Transition

Residential support for fulfilling lives in the community — from move-in costs to ongoing homemaker care.

Not sure which service fits?

Give us a call or send a question — we'll walk through your situation and figure out the right match.
