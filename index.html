Youth & Adults

Where You Need Us

At home, in the community, on the way to work or programming — wherever support is needed.

What We Offer

In-home and community support, built around each person.

Our team offers in-home care for adults plus community and in-home care for youth. Consult with us to explore everything we provide.

Personal Care

Bathing, dressing, grooming, mobility, medication reminders, and daily living support in the home.

Companionship

Housekeeping, meal preparation, conversation, and a friendly presence that keeps daily life moving.

Non-Medical Transportation

Rides to work, day programs, school, appointments, and errands — per-trip or per-mile rates available.

Adult Day Support

Community connection, life skills, and personal care for adults on IO, Level One, and SELF waivers.

Vocational Habilitation

Pre-employment skills — attention to task, problem solving, workplace conduct, and communication.

Shared Living

Adults with developmental disabilities live with a family member or caregiver who provides daily support.

Our Mission

A passion and readiness to serve.

Many people with disabilities have unique needs and capabilities. Our team meets each person where they are — whether that's finding reliable transportation to work, helping with healthy meals at home, or building skills for independent living. We're based in Eastlake and serve families across Ohio.

Who We Serve

Serving youth and adults with disabilities.

From children needing community-based support to adults navigating independent living, we tailor services to each person's goals, abilities, and waiver plan.

Adults with Disabilities

In-home personal care, companionship, day support, vocational habilitation, and employment support.

Youth & Children

Community and in-home care, residential respite, and connection-building activities.

Families & Caregivers

Shared Living arrangements, community respite, and a break when primary caregivers need it.

DODD Waiver Participants

Authorized services for people on Individual Options, Level One, and SELF waivers.
